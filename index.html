Dear neighbor,

Commons is the companion for this house and the common board for this community. One compact wall. One communication. Come home to it, or carry it on a phone — it is the same Commons.

At home it is the daily compass: weather, tides, radar, deliveries, and what is happening inside and outside the gates. In the clubhouse it is the community board — notices, minutes, events, meetings. What the desk publishes appears everywhere, without a second posting.

When a watch or warning is issued for this address, Commons takes command. Plain language. Radar and track. Whether to leave. The numbers that matter. NOAA radio stays on until the warning ends. When it is over, the board says so.

Common ground. Complete picture. Commons.

Sincerely,

Checklist

    How it works

    Kiosk

    Wall mode Use this on the always-on touch panel. Phone and laptop stay in browser mode.

    What kiosk changes Fills the screen, hides browser chrome habits, and keeps the rotation on. Uber and Instacart ideas will live here later. Address stays the one in Settings.

    Ideas

    Not built yet Ship something and it leaves this list. New ideas we agree on get added here.
    • Keep the Welcome letter current whenever a feature ships
    • Shared occupancy, vendors, and villa checks across every device
    • Delivery “delivered” checkbox that other screens see
    • Automatic BeachWalk / Englewood / Moose calendar wiring via Worker /ics
    • Commercial / clubhouse mode vs residential
    • Kiosk Uber: request a ride from this wall, or send a car to pick someone up. The kiosk does not move, so every trip starts or ends at the villa / clubhouse pin.
    • Kiosk Instacart: grocery order from the wall only, delivered to the same fixed kiosk address. Not a roaming phone cart.
    • Kiosk is a page, not an on/off button. That page holds setup and kiosk-only settings (pin address, rotation, dock, Uber/Instacart, what the wall is allowed to do).
    • Digital picture frame option: a quiet photo mode that cycles family or community pictures, then returns to the board. Settings for folder, timing, and whether it runs overnight or between rotations.

    Sources

    Where each number is collected. Model points follow the home address. Tide and radar stations are the nearest NOAA / NWS sites.

    Business

    Plan, price, markets, marketing, production, research. Not in rotation. Update as we learn.

    Plan

    What we are selling Commons is the companion and the community board. Common ground: weather that decides the day, and what the desk publishes. Compact on the clubhouse wall. Come home to the same board on a phone. BeachWalk by Manasota Key is the living pilot. Do not sell an HOA app. Do not sell a lobby slideshow.
    Wedge Start with one Florida HOA that already wants a clubhouse screen and will let us put a wall in a villa. Prove the storm takeover and the same events on both screens. Then sell the residential walls as a community package, not one-off gadgets.

    Pricing ideas

    Community unit Clubhouse or lobby. Working numbers to test, not a list price: hardware at cost or a thin markup, then a monthly community license in the $79–$199 range so a manager can expense it like a website add-on. Includes the desk publishing the newsletter, minutes, and alerts to every linked home.
    Residential wall Per home, billed through the association: something like $8–$15 / home / month on top of the community license, or a first-year hardware kit the HOA buys and a cheaper ongoing fee. Goal is “less than a streaming service, on the wall they already look at.” Never price it like a custom kiosk project.
    Hotel / Airbnb skin Property license, not per guest. Hotel: one lobby plus optional in-room. STR: host tablet plus guest-facing welcome. Higher setup, lower monthly than HOA because there is no association to bill. Keep owner flights and occupancy off this skin.
    What we will not charge for Do not sell “weather slides.” Do not nickel-and-dime radar, NOAA, or a warning. Storm mode is the product, not an add-on. Hardware replacement after a named storm is a goodwill / insurance conversation, not a surprise invoice.

    Markets

    Residential HOA / gated / villa Primary. Clubhouse television plus a welcome wall in each home. Minutes, newsletter, events, same weather and storm board. Buyer: manager or board. Status: building the product here first.
    Large condo and high-rise Lobby, mail room, elevator bank. Packages and meetings matter more than boat and cart. Competitors already sell lobby TVs. Status: pitch the unit on the wall inside the unit, not only the lobby.
    Garden apartments / multifamily Leasing office and clubhouse. Amenity hours, maintenance, weather. Buyer: regional PM. Status: later — they already buy resident apps.
    Active adult / 55+ Same as HOA, heavier on glanceability, radio, news, and storm prep. Status: strong fit. Do not make it look like a medical alert.
    Master-planned community One community unit, many neighborhoods. Pin is still the home address. Status: after the villa HOA works.
    Hotels and resorts Lobby and in-room outlook, beach and pool, live cam, local events. Brand as the property. Status: separate skin.
    Airbnb / short-term rental Host or cleaner tablet plus guest welcome wall. Open/close, Wi-Fi, beach, restaurants. Status: needs a guest mode switch.
    Clubhouse-only / amenity company Commercial unit with no homes yet. Foot in the door. Status: useful first sale.
    Marina / yacht club Marine first: tide, surge, small craft, lightning, NOAA. Status: after the water logic is trusted.

    Marketing

    One sentence Commons is the board the house and the neighborhood already look at — the daily outlook, and the same notice when the day changes.
    Where to show up Florida CAI chapters, CAM lunch-and-learns, clubhouse manager groups, hurricane-season webinars, local property-management offices. Demo on a real wall, not a slide deck. Lead with storm week, not features.
    Do not advertise as Alexa. Smart home. Digital signage package. Another resident app. A hurricane radio.

    Production

    Software One page, Cloudflare Worker, GitHub as the file of record. Drive automations for calendars, deliveries, flights. Address lives on the device. Community publishing still to be built so the desk can push without us.
    Hardware Always-on touch AIO with camera and speakers for the villa wall. Clubhouse can be a TV plus a cheap stick. Flush in-wall only if heat is solved. We do not become a hardware company — specify a kit, ship a buy list.
    Who runs it Desk updates newsletter and events. Commons updates weather and storms. We update the board. Resident does not have to learn a CMS.

    Research

    Closest products CondoSites Digital Information Display and BuildingLink Community Display prove boards will pay for a lobby screen with calendar plus a weather widget. Alosant and GateWatch own the app. Nobody ships residential + community, same language, weather that takes the rotation.
    Open questions Will an HOA buy walls for vacant snowbird months. Who owns the PIN and the address on a resale. Guest mode vs residential mode on one panel. Insurance and liability copy during a warning. Whether CAMs will publish minutes if it is one field instead of a PDF.
